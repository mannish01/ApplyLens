# ApplyLens — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

ApplyLens is an AI-powered opportunity intelligence
platform designed to help students discover,
evaluate, and manage opportunities.

## 2. Problem Statement

Students struggle to discover relevant opportunities,
understand eligibility requirements, extract information
from lengthy notices, and manage application deadlines.

## 3. Target Users

- Undergraduate students
- Postgraduate students
- Students searching for internships
- Students searching for scholarships
- Fresh graduates seeking entry-level jobs

## 4. Product Goals

- Simplify opportunity discovery.
- Help students understand eligibility.
- Extract useful information from PDF notices.
- Provide AI-powered document question answering.
- Organize and track applications.

## 5. MVP Features

1. Authentication
2. Student profile management
3. Opportunity discovery
4. Search and filtering
5. Save opportunities
6. Application tracking
7. PDF upload and text extraction
8. AI-based requirement extraction
9. Rule-based eligibility evaluation
10. Document question answering using RAG

## 6. AI Capabilities

- Extract structured requirements from notices.
- Explain eligibility using supporting evidence.
- Answer questions using retrieved document content.
- Provide references to relevant document pages.

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

- Secure authentication.
- Private document storage.
- Responsive user interface.
- Input validation.
- Protected user data.
- Reliable error handling.

## 8. Future Enhancements

- Resume matching
- Personalized recommendations
- Deadline notifications
- Preparation plans
- Advanced analytics

## 9. Success Criteria

- Students can register and maintain profiles.
- Students can discover and save opportunities.
- Students can track their applications.
- Students can upload and analyze PDF notices.
- Eligibility results include explanations and evidence.
- AI answers are grounded in uploaded documents.
